    Sources

    AI exposure share is the Brookings Institution's count of jobs exposed to generative AI divided by total jobs in the county. Prime-age (25–54) employment rate, the five-level distress ranking, local labor market names and county population are from the W.E. Upjohn Institute's Local Labor Market Distress Dataset. Employment by industry is from the BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages (QCEW), 2024 annual average (Connecticut, 2023).

    Classes

    AI exposure is split into terciles: . Labor markets are grouped by prime-age employment rate: healthy ≥ 80% (Upjohn's fully and close to fully employed), marginal 76.5–80% (marginally distressed) and distressed < 76.5% (distressed and severely distressed). Valdez-Cordova, AK and Kalawao, HI have no exposure data and Loving, TX has no employment rate; they are shown in gray.

    Industry mix

    Jobs are counted where people work, not where they live. Government is grouped by level, so public school teachers and public hospital staff count as government; active-duty military is excluded. "Not disclosed" is employment BLS withholds to protect individual employers. All jobs pools every job in the selection, so large counties dominate; Avg. county weights each county's mix equally. On the Largest industry map, stripes mark counties where withheld jobs exceed the lead of the largest sector over the second.
